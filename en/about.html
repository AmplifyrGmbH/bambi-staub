About Us

Three generations, one house, countless memories

History & Roots

Built with passion – carried on with love

In 1972, our parents Max and Mary Staub built Chalet Bambi – at a time when Sörenberg was still a quiet mountain village. They worked hard for every franc and poured everything into this dream. Much of it was built by their own hands – with care and a sense of quality that can still be felt today.

In 2018, the chalet underwent a thorough renovation – largely done ourselves, with the aim of preserving its original character. Today, responsibility lies with the families of the three sons. Maintenance, garden care and landscaping are handled by us wherever possible – with the same dedication that has defined this house from the very beginning.

When you visit Chalet Bambi, you won't just be renting a holiday home – you'll become part of a story that is still being written.

The Family

Family Staub – Your Hosts

We are the three sons of Mary and Hans Staub – and now fathers ourselves of nine children in total. Chalet Bambi means far more to us than a holiday home: it is the place where we grew up, where our children discover the mountains, and where generations come together. We are delighted to share this place with you.
