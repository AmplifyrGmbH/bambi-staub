Activities

Worth Experiencing Year-Round

❄

Winter

  • Skiing13 lifts between 1,165 m and 2,340 m – about 8–10 minutes on foot to the nearest lift. Slopes for all levels, suitable for children. Ski school offering lessons for young beginners.
  • Snowshoeing & Winter HikingMarked snowshoe routes through snowy forests and peat bog landscapes.
  • SledgingNatural toboggan runs for young and old – family-friendly winter fun.
  • Ice SkatingIce rink in the village – fun on ice for the whole family.
  • Cross-Country SkiingWell-groomed cross-country tracks for classic and skating – from the village square to the Rothorn railway and into the Saalwideli.
  • Ski & Snowboard LessonsSnow sports school right on-site – ideal for beginners and improving technique.
☀

Summer

  • HikingExtensive trail network through the Biosphere – from easy walks to challenging summit tours.
  • Cycling RoutesVaried cycling routes through forests and alpine meadows for all abilities.
  • Bog Worlds at RossweidUnique bog experience on the "Mooraculum" adventure trail – nature wonders up close.
  • Summer Toboggan Run RischliThrills on the summer toboggan run – fun for the whole family.
  • BBQ Spots & PicnicsNumerous barbecue and picnic areas in the midst of nature.
◆

Year-Round

  • Brienzer Rothorn RailwayThe steam cog railway to the Rothorn (2,348 m) – an unforgettable experience with panoramic views of the Alps since 1892.
  • UNESCO BiosphereExcursions, courses and guided experiences in one of Switzerland's most species-rich regions.
  • Local ProduceFresh alpine cheese, Entlebuch specialities and regional cuisine direct from the producer.

Dining

Our Restaurant Recommendations

Alpenrösli

Family-friendly restaurant in the village – certified family-friendly with a children's menu and play area. A relaxed atmosphere to linger.

Visit website ↗

Kurhaus Flühli

Historic spa hotel with original preserved tiled stove and rustic dining rooms. Seasonal, regional cuisine from timeless classics to Mediterranean reinterpretations.

Visit website ↗

Rischli

Modern cuisine at a refined level – over 25% of ingredients sourced from the UNESCO Biosphere Entlebuch. Creative combinations of classic and modern cooking techniques.

Visit website ↗

Treffpunkt

Traditional home cooking, fine desserts and aromatic coffees – with a hunting-themed snug and sunny terrace with mountain views. A meeting point for locals and guests alike.

Visit website ↗

Bergwelten Salwideli

Regional specialities at 1,800 m in a cosy setting. Famous for the Tomahawk steak, served on a block of Schratten limestone.

Visit website ↗
