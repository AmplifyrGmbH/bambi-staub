Rooms & Amenities

Rooms

Chalet Atmosphere

01

Entrance

The entrance welcomes you with typical chalet charm – warm wood and character.

02

WC & Shower

Toilet with shower – well-maintained and fully equipped.

03

Living & Dining Room

Warm-air stove with thermostat, dining table with corner bench, cosy sofa and sofa bed – the perfect place for shared evenings.

04

Kitchen

Fully equipped kitchen with Nespresso machine and everything you need – including fondue set and raclette oven for authentic Swiss evenings.

05

Bedroom

The bedroom offers 4 bunk beds each with a power outlet – ideal for families with children. A hatch with alternating-step stairs leads down to the finished lower level.

06

Lower Level

The lower level offers additional living space with a sofa, armchairs and TV. Access from the bedroom via a hatch with alternating-step stairs – or from outside via the garden stairs.

07

Sun Terrace

The 20 m² south-facing sun terrace offers views of the Brienzer Rothorn and the Waldemme river. Perfect for breakfast in the sunshine or relaxed evenings outdoors.

08

Garden & Outdoor

650 m² of private grounds: seating area with table and garden chairs, fire pit and stone fireplace. Direct access to the stream – children can play there under supervision at low water levels.
