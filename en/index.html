Surroundings

In the Heart of the UNESCO Biosphere Entlebuch

Sörenberg sits at 1,160 m above sea level at the foot of the Brienzer Rothorn. The region is part of the UNESCO Biosphere Entlebuch – a unique mosaic of peat bogs, alpine meadows and ancient forests.
