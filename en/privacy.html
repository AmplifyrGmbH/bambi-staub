Web Analytics with Umami

This website uses Umami, a privacy-friendly web analytics tool. Umami does not collect personal data, sets no cookies, and is fully compliant with GDPR and the Swiss DSG.

Umami records only anonymous usage statistics: pages visited, time on site, country of origin (based on IP, which is not stored), browser and device type. No user profile is created and no information is shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Provider: Umami Software Inc., cloud.umami.is. For more information see umami.is/privacy.

Collection and Processing of Other Data

Beyond the web analytics described above, no further personal data is stored unless you actively contact us by e-mail.

E-mail Contact

If you contact us by e-mail, the data you provide (name, e-mail address, message content) will be used solely to process your enquiry and will not be passed on to third parties.

Google Maps

Some pages of this website embed a map from Google Maps. When this map loads, your browser transmits data to Google LLC (USA). For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Fonts

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA). Your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process. We use Google Fonts for design reasons. Google's privacy policy can be found at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your Rights

You have the right at any time to request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of your personal data held by us. Please contact us by e-mail at info@bambi-staub.ch.

Changes to this Privacy Policy
