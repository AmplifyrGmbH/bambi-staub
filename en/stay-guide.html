Arrival

Four steps from setting off to your first coffee on the terrace.

  1. Getting here

    By car

    From Lucerne via Malters, Wolhusen and Entlebuch to Schüpfheim, then via Flühli to Sörenberg. Approx. 1 hour.

    By public transport

    Train to Schüpfheim, then PostBus line 241 via Flühli. Get off at Höchistrasse – 50 m from the chalet. Approx. 1 hour from Lucerne.

    Open in Google Maps

  2. Parking

    Rothornstrasse 40, 6174 Sörenberg.

    Two free outdoor parking spaces right in front of the chalet.

  3. Collect the key

    Check-in from 15:00. The key is in the key safe at the entrance.

    You will receive the code by email in good time before you arrive.

  4. Pay the tourist tax

    Within the first few days at the Sörenberg Tourist Office, Rothornstrasse 21 (stop: Post). Mon–Fri 8–12 and 13.30–17.

    CHF 4.– per person aged 16+ per night, half in the off-season, younger guests free.

In the chalet

How the heating, hot water and appliances work.

Heating

The oil-fired warm-air heaters regulate the temperature automatically – simply set the temperature you want.

  • Living room thermostat: on the wall towards the entrance
  • Basement thermostat: inside the right-hand heater door
Heater makes noise but doesn't get warm?

Open the right-hand door (instructions inside) and check:

  1. Are all switches in the correct position?
  2. Are all fuses in the heater door present? (bright filament = intact)
  3. Is the thin V-belt still over both drive wheels (large at the top, small at the bottom)?
  4. At the arrow on the left (back, bottom): has the regulator latch clicked down? Move it up and down a few times, then press it down.

Still not working: Mr Kaufmann, 079 786 29 52.

Hot water

The boiler (120 litres) heats up automatically between 2 and 6 am – setting «Night». Please leave it like this.

If hot water runs low: set the switch to the left of the stove to «Day» for 2–4 hours, then back to «Night».

Shower room

Electric wall heater with timer: ON/OFF (6) → Timer (1) → set hours with + (4) → wait 3 seconds. Heats up in about 1 hour.

Please air the room well after showering.

Kitchen

  • Ceramic hob with 4 zones, oven below (dial). Please clean after use.
  • Kitchen linen is provided.
  • Before leaving, empty the fridge, set it to 0 (press for a few seconds) and leave the door open.

Beds & linen

  • Please bring bed linen or rent it when booking (CHF 25.–/person). Guests make the beds themselves.
  • Nordic duvets 160 × 210 cm, pillows 65 × 100 cm.
  • Sofa bed in the living room (160 × 200 cm): please bring a fitted sheet and bed linen.
  • Wool blankets in the chest in the bedroom.

TV & radio

TV with satellite reception (Astra). To switch off: hold On/Off for a few seconds. Swiss channels appear after about 1 minute.

DAB+ radio with CD, USB, Bluetooth and remote control. No Wi-Fi in the chalet.

Fire bowl & barbecue

Place the fire bowl on the three bricks at the seating area – never use it under the balcony. Firewood is free to use.

Safety

Fire extinguisher in the basement next to the stairs, fire blanket in the entrance area.

Waste & recycling

Recycling

Collection point 50 m away, next to the petrol station: glass, PET, metal, batteries, paper, cardboard and general waste.

General waste

General waste needs waste stickers – available at Volg Sörenberg.

Compost

Compost bin in the garden for vegetables, coffee grounds and eggshells. No meat, no cooked food, no charcoal or half-burnt wood.
