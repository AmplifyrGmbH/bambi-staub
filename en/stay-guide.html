Chalet Bambi at a Glance

Built in 1972. Idyllically situated on a 650 m² plot directly on the Waldemme river – with a direct view of the Brienzer Rothorn.

Living area 35 m² living space + 27 m² finished basement
Sleeping places 4 bunk beds + 1 sofa bed (1.60 × 2.00 m)
Capacity Ideal for 4 persons
Terrace Large sun terrace with views of the Waldemme and Brienzer Rothorn
Postal address Rothornstrasse 40, Postfach 459, 6174 Sörenberg

Arrival & Departure

Getting here

By car

A2 towards Lucerne, exit Wolhusen, then via Entlebuch to Flühli and Sörenberg. Approx. 40 min from Lucerne.

Open in Google Maps →

By public transport

Train via Entlebuch to Schüpfheim, then Entlebuch rail towards Sörenberg, followed by PostBus to Sörenberg. Alight at stop Höchistrasse – just 50 m from the chalet.

Address & Parking

Rothornstrasse 40, 6174 Sörenberg. Two free outdoor parking spaces directly in front of the chalet.

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in from 15:00

You will find the key in the key safe at the entrance. The code will be sent to you by e-mail in good time before your arrival.

Please pay the tourist tax within the first few days at the Sörenberg Tourist Office (stop: Post).

Check-out by 12:00

Return the key to the safe and reset the code to 0000.

  • Close all windows & shutters, leave interior doors open
  • Turn off lights, switch off the TV
  • Set boiler to 0
  • Set fridge to 0 (leave door open)
  • Set both warm-air heaters to 5 degrees

Heating

The oil-powered warm-air heaters regulate the temperature automatically – simply set your desired temperature.

Thermostat living room On the wall towards the entrance area
Thermostat basement Inside the right heater door

If the heater makes noise but doesn't heat up:

Open the right door (the manual is inside) and check the following:

  1. Are all switches in the correct position?
  2. Are all fuses in the heater door intact? (Glowing wire = intact)
  3. Is the thin v-belt still running over the two drive wheels (large on top, small on bottom)?
  4. At the arrow on the left side (rear bottom): is the regulator "flap" snapped downward? Move it up and down a few times and then press it down so it no longer springs up.

If none of this helps, please contact Mr Kaufmann (see Important Numbers).

Hot Water & Boiler

Standard – "Night" setting

The boiler (120 litres) heats up automatically between 02:00 and 06:00. This is the normal setting – please leave it as is.

Extra demand – "Day" setting

If hot water runs short: set the switch to the left of the hob to "Day" for 2–4 hours. Afterwards return it to "Night".

Shower room – Electric wall heater

Wall heater with timer: ON/OFF (6) → Timer (1) → Set hours with + (4) → Wait 3 seconds. Heating time approx. 1 hour.

Please ventilate thoroughly after showering.

Bed Linen

Bed linen

Kitchen linen is provided. All other linen please bring yourself. Duvets: Nordic 1.60 × 2.10 m, pillows 65 × 100 cm.

Bed linen on request: CHF 20.–/bed – please indicate when booking.

Sofa bed

A sofa bed is available in the living room (1.60 × 2.00 m). Please bring your own fitted sheet and bed linen.

Woollen blankets

If needed, you will find woollen blankets in the chest in the bedroom.

Kitchen & Appliances

Hob & Oven

Ceramic hob with 4 zones. Oven below – set temperature with the dial. Please clean after use.

Fridge

Please empty before departure. Set fridge to 0 (press for a few seconds) and leave the door open.

Fire safety

Fire extinguisher in the basement next to the stairs. Fire blanket in the entrance area.

TV

Television with satellite reception (Astra). To switch off: hold On/Off for several seconds. Swiss channels connect after approx. 1 minute.

Radio

DAB+ radio with CD, USB, Bluetooth and remote control. No Wi-Fi in the chalet.

Fire bowl

Do not use under the balcony. Place on the three bricks at the seating area.

House Rules

  • Smoking: The entire chalet is non-smoking. Please smoke outside only.
  • Pets: Not permitted.
  • Quiet hours: 22:00–07:00.
  • Occupancy: Unregistered guests staying overnight must be registered (tourist tax applies, no extra charge).
  • Cleaning: Please leave the chalet tidy: dishes washed, oven clean, fridge emptied, bed linen piled up, rubbish disposed of.
  • Damage: Please report any damage or defects promptly.

Waste & Recycling

Recycling

Recycling station 50 m away – next to the petrol station. Glass, PET, metal, batteries, paper, cardboard and general waste.

Compost

Compost bin in the garden for vegetables, coffee grounds, eggshells. No meat, no cooked food, no coal or half-burnt wood.

General waste

Waste stickers are required for general rubbish (available at Volg Sörenberg).
