UNESCO Biosphere Entlebuch

One of the Most Species-Rich
Regions in Switzerland

The Entlebuch has been recognised as a UNESCO Biosphere since 2001 – one of the first biospheres worldwide created through a popular vote. Vast peat bogs, deep forests and majestic Alpine ridges define this unique landscape. Sörenberg sits at 1,160 m above sea level right in its heart.

Brienzer Rothorn

Sörenberg's iconic home mountain offers a spectacular panorama over the pre-Alps and the Swiss Plateau. Reachable by cable car in minutes – a must in every season.

Natural Gem

Peat Bog Landscapes

The Entlebuch is home to some of Switzerland's largest intact raised bogs. Rare flora and fauna, quiet hamlets and a trail through the moors – pure nature right on your doorstep.

Sörenberg

Village & Infrastructure

Restaurants, ski area, gondola, grocery shops – all within a few minutes' walk. Sörenberg is a compact mountain village with everything you need, without losing its alpine character.

Since 1892

The Steam Cog Railway

One of the last steam cog railways in the world has been climbing the Brienzer Rothorn for over 130 years. The journey through forests, alpine pastures and rock faces up to 2,244 m is a unique experience – timeless, majestic and unforgettable.

The valley station in Brienz is about 60 minutes from Chalet Bambi. The railway operates seasonally from May to October. At the top, a panorama awaits you that encompasses all of Central Switzerland.

The Landscape

Peaks, Forests & Water

Schratteflue 2,091 m a.s.l.

Striking limestone massif with a unique karst landscape. A popular destination for experienced mountain hikers – partly exposed ridges with sweeping views across the Entlebuch.

Haglere 1,948 m a.s.l.

A gentler summit with panoramic views over Sörenberg and the surrounding valleys. Easily accessible and popular with families – walk on from the gondola at Rossweid.

Source of the Waldemme c. 1,900 m a.s.l.

Just 3 km from the chalet, the Waldemme springs up – a quiet, powerful experience in untouched nature. An ideal destination for a peaceful walk.

Rossweid & Moorakulum 1,600 m a.s.l.

The gondola to Rossweid leads directly into the peat bog – the "Moorakulum" brings the fascinating world of raised bogs to life for children and adults alike.
